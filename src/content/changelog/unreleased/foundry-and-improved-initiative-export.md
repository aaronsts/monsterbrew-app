---
title: Export to Foundry and Improved Initiative
---

Your creatures can now leave Monsterbrew as FoundryVTT actors or Improved Initiative statblocks. Every export option sits behind a single Export button on a creature's page, instead of one button per format.

- Export a creature as a FoundryVTT actor, built for the dnd5e system
- Attacks and saving throws arrive in Foundry as rollable actions, not just text
- Export to Improved Initiative, which Monsterbrew can also read back in
- Homebrewery markdown and PDF moved into the same dialog, otherwise unchanged
