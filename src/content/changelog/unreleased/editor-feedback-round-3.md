Another round of editor fixes from your feedback. Combobox fields play nicer when they're already filled in, hovering creatures render properly, and the CR suggestions got smarter about which ability actually drives attacks.

- Reopening a filled-in type or size dropdown shows all the options again instead of only the one you picked
- Picking a creature type adds a small info icon next to the field — hover it to read what that type covers
- A flying creature that can hover reads “Fly 60 ft. (hover)” on the statblock and in exports, instead of a stray “Hover true”
- CR suggestions no longer project the attack bonus from Constitution — no attacks are linked to it, so the badge lands on the ability that actually swings
- The CR suggestion badge sits under the ability score instead of crowding its label
- The unsaved-changes reminder now returns every 30 seconds until the creature is saved
