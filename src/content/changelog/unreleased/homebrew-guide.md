---
title: Creature building guide
---

Monsterbrew now ships with a full guide to creating homebrew monsters for the 2024 rules. Seven chapters take you from first concept to playtested creature: what makes a monster fun, how challenge rating really maps to AC, HP, and damage (with a complete benchmark table for CR 0–30), how to budget offense and defense, how to write traits that create decisions at the table, and the classic pitfalls to avoid. It consolidates the best of the community’s design knowledge — the Lazy GM’s Monster Builder Resource Document, Blog of Holding’s 2024 Monster Manual analysis, and Alphastream’s creation process — with full credit to the original creators.

- New Guide section in the navigation, with seven chapters you can read in order or jump between
- A complete monster-statistics-by-CR reference table for CR 0 through 30, tuned to the 2024 books
- Quick formulas for improvising a balanced monster in seconds
- Practical fixes for HP sponges, stun-locks, overloaded statblocks, and lonely solo bosses
- Chapters link straight into the editor and SRD library so you can apply the advice as you build
