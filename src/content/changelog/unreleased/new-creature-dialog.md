---
title: Choose your base to start from
---

Opening the editor with nothing loaded used to drop you straight into an empty form full of zeros. It now asks where you want to begin.

- Start blank, exactly as before, and the chooser gets out of the way
- Start from an existing creature: one of your own, or any of the 329 SRD monsters, searchable by name and filterable by source, size, type, and challenge rating, without leaving the editor
- Building on one of your own creatures makes a copy, so the original is left alone
- Import or paste a creature from the same place, instead of hunting for the button in the toolbar
- Jump back into one of your recently edited creatures
- Press Escape, or click outside, to skip the whole thing and start blank

Opening the editor also no longer flashes an empty page. The form and statblock now have a placeholder while the page loads, instead of a blank gap under the header.
