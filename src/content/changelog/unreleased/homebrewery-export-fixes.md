A round of fixes for the statblock preview and Homebrewery export. Bold text no longer leaves a stray gap before the punctuation right after it, the Homebrewery export now includes the Initiative line the 2024 stat blocks need, and multi-word skills like Sleight of Hand and Animal Handling capitalize correctly everywhere.

- Fixed a stray space appearing after bold text in the statblock preview, e.g. "Poisoned !" instead of "Poisoned!"
- Homebrewery exports now include an Initiative line, matching the 2024 stat block format
- Sleight of Hand and Animal Handling now capitalize correctly in both the preview and Homebrewery exports
