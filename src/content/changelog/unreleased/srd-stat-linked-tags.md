SRD monsters are no longer frozen text. Their attack lines, saving throws, and damage clauses are now built from the same stat-linked tags your own creatures use, so when you copy one to the editor and start tweaking, the numbers follow along. Raise the Aboleth's Strength and its tentacle's to-hit updates by itself; damage averages recompute from the dice as you change them.

- Attack bonuses on SRD monsters are linked to the ability that produces them, so they update when you change the score
- Save DCs know their source ability too, including tricky ones like a dragon's breath DC coming from Constitution behind a Dexterity save
- Damage averages are computed live from the dice instead of being fixed text
- Attack and save lines on copied SRD monsters open in the click-to-edit dialog, just like lines you write yourself
- SRD attack lines now show the official “Hit:” label before damage
- Everything else renders exactly as it did before — only the numbers became live
