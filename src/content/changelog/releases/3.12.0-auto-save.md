Your edits are safer now. The creature editor saves your changes automatically as you work, so switching tabs or closing the page no longer risks losing unsaved edits. A subtle "Saving… / All changes saved" indicator next to the Save button shows where you stand.

- Auto-save persists valid edits to an already-saved creature about a second after you stop typing; the manual Save/Update button still works as before
- A save-state indicator shows "Saving…" or "All changes saved" in the editor toolbar
- Still-unsaved new creatures get a gentle reminder to Save so auto-save can take over
- Loading a creature no longer risks a background refresh quietly overwriting your in-progress edits
