A second round of editor improvements from your feedback. The combat section now starts with ability scores, since everything else derives from them, and passive perception moved right next to them instead of hiding among the senses. Trait and action fields got their own example placeholders, and the attack and saving-throw helpers became a bit friendlier.

- Ability scores now sit at the top of the combat section, with passive perception right after them
- New creatures start with 2 hit dice, so the statblock shows hit points right away
- The skills list now explains its two checkboxes: one square is proficient, two is expertise
- Every section (traits, actions, reactions, …) has its own example placeholders instead of all showing “ex. Multiattack”
- Traits no longer offer the attack and saving-throw insert buttons — those belong on actions
- The attack and saving-throw popups have a clear close button
- Damage is now clearly optional on saving-throw lines — leave the dice empty for save-or-suck effects
- A name or description starting with a space no longer breaks the bold formatting on the statblock
- The AC description (like “natural armor”) now actually shows on the statblock next to the armor class
- Hit point formulas no longer show a pointless “+ 0” when the constitution modifier is zero
- The arrows on speed and sense fields now step in increments of 5 ft.
- New description field for flavor text — it shows at the bottom of the statblock and in Homebrewery exports
