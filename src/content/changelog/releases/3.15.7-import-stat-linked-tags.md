Imported creatures are no longer frozen text either. When you import from Improved Initiative, TetraCube, or Open5e, attack lines, saving throws, and damage clauses become the same live, stat-linked lines the SRD monsters use, so the numbers follow along when you tweak the creature in the editor.

- To-hit bonuses and save DCs link to the ability score that produces them
- Damage averages recompute live from the dice as you change them
- Both the classic 2014 wording and the newer 2024 wording are recognized
- Anything the import can't verify stays exactly as it was written
- TetraCube files with a challenge rating like “23 (50,000 XP)” used to import at CR 0; they now keep their real rating
