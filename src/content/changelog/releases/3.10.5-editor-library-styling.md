The editor and your library got a visual tidy-up. Each editor section can now be folded away, so you can focus on the part you're working on instead of scrolling past everything else. And on a creature's page in your library, the row of buttons is now a single tidy Actions menu. Statblock text also sits a little tighter, closer to the printed-book look.

- Editor sections (Identity, Combat, Defense, Actions) now collapse and expand — fold away the parts you're done with
- The creature page gathers Edit, Duplicate, Export (Homebrewery markdown or PDF) and Delete into one Actions menu
- Delete still asks for confirmation first, and now shows in a proper warning red inside the menu
- Statblocks use tighter line spacing, so more of your creature fits on screen at once
